# Interpretation of the simulation

The large-cluster target is the treatment effect at the population reference score 90, equal to 5 in both specified outcome models. The small-cluster target is the expected first-versus-second outcome difference, 7.727272727, which includes the baseline component 2.727272727. They are different objects.

Independent applicant records, continuous scores, a predetermined 10 percent quota, independent Gaussian noise, full outcome observation, no interference, and a stable population hold by construction. Their plausibility for an actual programme is not assessed here.

Adding independent 10-person clusters improves precision for the adjacent comparison without eliminating its expected untreated-outcome difference. Other information or restrictions may support other estimators; this simulation is not an impossibility result for all small-cluster causal inference.

HC0 normal intervals follow the manuscript's estimator. The main DGP has a linear untreated mean and a constant effect. Coverage is reported as generated, with Monte Carlo error. No exact finite-sample or automatic-bandwidth guarantee is claimed. The known baseline gap in synthetic data is not automatically observable in real data.
